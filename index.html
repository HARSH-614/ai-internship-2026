Agenda

1. Core Foundations

  • 1950: Alan Turing proposes the "Turing Test"
  • 1956: John McCarthy coins "Artificial Intelligence"
  • Types: Narrow, General, Super AI

2. The Learning Hierarchy

Artificial Intelligence
(Machines simulating intellect)

↓

Machine Learning
(Learning from data without explicit rules)

↓

Deep Learning
(Multi-layered neural networks)

3. Prompt Engineering

Mastering the C-R-I-S-P Framework:

  • Context & Role
  • Instruction & Specifics
  • Parameters

Techniques: Zero-Shot, One-Shot, Few-Shot, Role-Based.

4. AI in Education & Media

  • Lesson Planning: E.g., Structuring 45-min Physics lessons on BJT/Semiconductors.
  • Image (DALL-E): S-S-L-R formula (Subject, Style, Lighting, Resolution).
  • Video/Audio: Runway ML, Pika Labs, ElevenLabs voice cloning.

5. Responsible AI

  • Hallucinations: Verifying facts to prevent false outputs.
  • Deepfakes: Mitigating risks of manipulated video/audio.
  • Copyright: Ensuring original content creation and fair use.

Conclusion

AI is a powerful co-pilot for development, research, and design.

Human Intelligence + AI Assistance = Limitless Potential.
